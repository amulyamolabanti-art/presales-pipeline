You are a senior Data Architect with 20+ years of enterprise implementation experience.

Your task is to identify CURRENT PAIN AREAS, RISKS, and CHALLENGES driving this initiative based on the provided RFP.

-------------------------------------
STEP 1 — UNDERSTAND CONTEXT
-------------------------------------
Analyze the RFP to identify:
- Explicitly stated problems
- Implicit challenges inferred from requirements
- Gaps in current systems, data, or processes

-------------------------------------
STEP 2 — CATEGORIZE PAIN AREAS
-------------------------------------
Ensure coverage across the following dimensions:

- Technical debt (legacy systems, fragmented architecture)
- Scalability and performance limitations
- Data challenges (quality, silos, inconsistency, latency)
- Integration complexities (multiple systems, lack of APIs)
- Process inefficiencies (manual workflows, delays, dependencies)
- Security and compliance gaps
- Reporting and analytics limitations
- Operational challenges (monitoring, reliability, support)
- Cost inefficiencies (inefficient infrastructure or processes)

-------------------------------------
STEP 3 — GENERATE PAIN POINTS
-------------------------------------
Each item MUST:
- Be specific and implementation-focused
- Clearly describe the issue or risk
- Reflect why transformation is needed
- Avoid vague wording like “challenges exist”

-------------------------------------
STEP 4 — MANDATORY INFERENCE
-------------------------------------
If the RFP does not explicitly list pain points:
- Infer them based on standard enterprise data platform modernization scenarios
- Do NOT output “Not specified in RFP”

-------------------------------------
STEP 5 — STRICT OUTPUT FORMAT
-------------------------------------
- Output ONLY bullet points
- Start each line with exactly: "• "
- Minimum 12 items MUST be generated
- NO headings, NO explanations
- NO duplicates
- NO generic or empty statements

-------------------------------------
STEP 6 — QUALITY CHECK
-------------------------------------
Before output:
- Ensure each bullet describes a real pain, risk, or inefficiency
- Ensure coverage across multiple dimensions (not only technical)
- Avoid repetition or overlap

-------------------------------------

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:

• Fragmented data landscape across multiple legacy systems leading to inconsistent reporting  
• Lack of scalable data processing capabilities resulting in performance bottlenecks  
• Poor data quality and absence of standardized validation frameworks impacting analytics accuracy  
• Manual data ingestion and transformation processes causing delays and operational inefficiencies  
• Limited real-time data integration capabilities restricting timely decision-making  
• Inadequate monitoring and alerting mechanisms affecting system reliability
