# Sample RFPs

This directory contains **synthetic** (fully simulated / mock) Request for Proposal (RFP) and RFI documents used for local testing, prompt prototyping, chunking evaluation, and retrieval benchmarking.

> **Note:** These documents are 100% synthetic and do not contain or derive from any real client or customer proposals.

## Included Synthetic Samples
1. `SAMPLE_RFP_01_Harborline_Claims_Modernisation.docx` - Synthetic insurance claims modernization RFP
2. `SAMPLE_RFI_02_Cedarbrook_Data_Platform.pdf` - Synthetic enterprise data platform RFI
3. `SAMPLE_RFP_03_Velora_Search_Managed_Services.pdf` - Synthetic search & managed services RFP

## Guidelines
- **Formats supported:** `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`, `.ppt`, `.txt`.
- **Security & Compliance:** Any future additions must remain strictly synthetic or thoroughly scrubbed of real client PII, confidential pricing, or proprietary data.
- **Usage:**
  - Prototype document ingestion with Document Intelligence / chunking pipelines.
  - Test retrieval and citation accuracy.
  - Benchmark drafting and compliance agents.
